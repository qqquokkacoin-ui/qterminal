WHY WE BUILT THIS

We've been trading stocks and crypto for a long time. For the first time, with tokenization, these two worlds are finally starting to merge. Yet there's a real lack of analytical tools to help traders make informed decisions when it comes to trading perps.

So we made one ourselves.

THE TERMINAL

Qterminal is a Bloomberg-terminal-style dashboard for Robinhood's tokenized QQQ and every tokenized NASDAQ-100 stock — live prices, interactive charts, real fundamentals, news, and a sector-by-sector heatmap of the whole index, all in one place.

PREMIUM ACCESS

Premium features unlock by connecting your wallet and simply holding $QTRM — no burning, no locking anything up. Hold enough in a connected wallet and premium unlocks automatically.

On-chain activity
Deeper live on-chain analytics beyond the standard heatmap and charts.
Portfolio backtester
Build, backtest, and track custom tokenized-stock baskets against QQQ.

THE COIN

Holding $QTRM in a wallet connected to your account does more than unlock premium — it earns you rewards, paid out in the tokenized stock of your choice. You pick which stock you'd like your rewards in.
